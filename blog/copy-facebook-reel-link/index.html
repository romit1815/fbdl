How to copy a Facebook Reel link on a phone or computer

Copy the direct link to a Facebook Reel on mobile or desktop, check that it opens the right post, and avoid common link-copy mistakes.

Home › Blog › How to copy a Facebook Reel link on a phone or computer

25 October 2026 · FBviddl guide

A downloader can only work with the address you give it. The most reliable starting point is the Reel’s own share link, then a quick check that the address opens the correct video. Facebook’s labels can shift between app versions, but the basic process is the same: open the Reel, choose its sharing option, and copy the link.

Copy a Reel link in the Facebook app

  1. Open Facebook and navigate to the Reel you want to save.
  2. Open the Reel itself so it is the active post, not just a thumbnail in a profile grid.
  3. Tap the Share control. Depending on the app version, you may need to open the more-options menu first.
  4. Choose the clipboard command or its closest equivalent. This sends the URL to your device clipboard.
  5. Return to the Reel downloader, paste the address, and confirm that the preview shows the intended clip before continuing.

If your phone opens its sharing panel first, swipe through the available actions and choose the clipboard command. Avoid selecting a messaging app unless you intend to send the link to someone.

Copy a Reel link on a computer

  1. Open Facebook in your browser and click the Reel so its post page is visible.
  2. In the sharing panel, choose the clipboard action. Alternatively, open the Reel fully and copy its URL from the browser address bar.
  3. Paste the link into a text field temporarily if you want to confirm you copied a full address beginning with https://.
  4. Submit it in the Reel downloader and verify that the returned preview is the same Reel.

Copying from the browser bar is useful when the share menu is unavailable. Make sure the address belongs to the Reel page and not to a search-results page or the creator’s profile.

Check the link before you submit it

Paste the address into a new browser tab first if you are unsure. It should open the intended Reel without requiring you to guess which post it points to. A valid-looking URL can still lead to the wrong item, so use the preview as your final confirmation.

Facebook may add tracking or sharing parameters to a link. Leave Facebook’s full share address intact; removing parameters can stop its redirect from reaching the post. Short links can redirect to a longer post address; that does not automatically mean the link is broken.

Common links that are easy to confuse

  • Profile address: takes you to a person or Page, not to one specific Reel.
  • Photo or album address: belongs in an image workflow, not the Reel downloader.
  • Comment address: may open a discussion but not identify the video itself.
  • Copied caption text: is not a link, even when it includes a creator name or hashtag.
  • Private share link: can load through your account but remain unavailable to outside visitors.

For a profile portrait or a single picture from an album, read the photo address walkthrough.

When the copied link does not open

Return to the Reel and copy its address again from the post controls. Then test it in a separate browser profile with no Facebook session. If the page does not resolve to the same video, the post may have moved, been removed, or had its audience changed. For other fixes, read the Reel download checklist.

Quick answers

Can I paste a Facebook profile link instead?

A profile address points to a person or Page, not a particular Reel. Open the video post itself and copy its address.

Should I shorten the address before submitting it?

Keep the address as Facebook supplied it. Some extra-looking parts help route a shared URL to its post.
