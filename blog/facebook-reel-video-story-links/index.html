Facebook Reel, video, Story, or Live: choose the right link

Learn how Facebook Reels, regular videos, Stories and Live replays differ, which link to copy, and when a post may not be publicly available.

Home › Blog › Facebook Reel, video, Story, or Live: choose the right link

25 October 2026 · FBviddl guide

Facebook can show several kinds of moving media in the same feed. A Reel, a longer video post, a Story, and a Live broadcast may look similar in a screenshot, but they do not always have the same link or availability. Identify the post type first so you can copy the right address and set realistic expectations.

Reel or regular video post?

A Reel is presented as a short-form video post. A regular video may be shared as a standalone feed post, attached to a Page, or embedded in another post. The best clue is the page Facebook opens when you tap the item: copy the address from that specific post rather than using the creator’s profile URL.

For a Reel, use the Reel downloader. If the post is a different type of public video and the Reel tool does not recognize it, try the main Facebook video downloader with the direct post link. Always check the preview before downloading.

How Stories differ

Stories are designed as temporary posts and may disappear from public view after their display period or when the author removes them. A Story link that worked earlier can therefore stop opening. Stories can also have a limited audience, so being able to view one while signed in does not mean it is publicly accessible.

If the Story is yours, check Facebook’s own archive and account options. If it belongs to someone else, ask the author for permission and a copy instead of working around the creator’s audience choice.

Live video and replays

A Live broadcast is a real-time event. Afterward, the author may leave a replay, edit it, change its audience, or remove it. Treat the replay as its own post: copy the link from the version that is currently visible. A link to an event page or the creator’s profile may not identify the replay.

Check access before troubleshooting

Try the URL in a separate browser profile with no Facebook account active. An authentication screen or an unavailable notice means that outside visitors cannot currently reach the post. Read the guide to privacy and public links before deciding what to try next.

Quick answers

Can a Reel address identify every Facebook video?

No. Copy the URL from the item you want, then use the page designed for that type of post. Profile, Story, and photo addresses do not identify a Reel.

Why did a Story address stop opening?

Stories can disappear or have their visibility narrowed. Once the author removes one or limits its audience, a link tool cannot bring it back.
