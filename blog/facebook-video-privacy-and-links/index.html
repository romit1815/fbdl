Can you download private Facebook videos?

Understand why Facebook audience settings affect video links, how to check public access, and what to do when a post is private or restricted.

Home › Blog › Can you download private Facebook videos?

25 October 2026 · FBviddl guide

A video opening in your Facebook app does not necessarily mean that anyone with its link can view it. Your signed-in account may have permission through a friends-only audience, group membership, or another access setting. This guide explains a simple way to check a link and what to do when the video is not public.

What “publicly accessible” means for a link

For a link-based request, public accessibility means the post can be opened without relying on your personal Facebook session or membership in a restricted audience. A public profile can still contain posts with narrower audiences, so check the specific video rather than the whole profile.

Facebook lets people adjust settings for posts, Reels, Stories, followers, and other parts of their account. Its privacy settings guide describes the controls available in Facebook; labels and options may vary by profile.

Check the exact post while signed out

  1. Copy the URL from the video post itself.
  2. Open a second browser profile that has no active Facebook session.
  3. Enter the address and check whether Facebook displays the video.
  4. If the site presents an authentication screen, an unavailable notice, or takes you to a profile without the post, outside visitors cannot currently reach that video.

This check only describes access now. The author can later change the audience or remove the post.

Why a public profile does not make every post public

People can choose an audience for individual posts. A video can also appear in a private group, be shared from another person’s post, or include audience rules connected to tagged people. The profile’s general visibility does not override those post-level choices.

If the post appears through your account but not through a separate browser profile, ask its author for access or a copy instead of trying to get around the setting. Do not try to work around a private setting with another account or a technical trick.

What to do if the video is private

  • Ask the person who posted it to share a copy with you or change the audience if they choose.
  • For a video you posted, request an account archive or use a backup you already keep.
  • If you manage the Page, double-check the post address and review its audience selector.
  • If the video was removed, use your own backup or request a copy from its owner.

FBviddl works with supported, accessible links. It cannot grant permission, sign in as you, or recover content that is not available.

Access and permission are different questions

Being able to view a public video is not the same as having permission to repost it, edit it, or use it commercially. Before saving or sharing someone else’s work, consider the creator’s rights and the reason you need the copy. For an overview of link types, read which Facebook video link to use.

Quick answers

Why can I see a video but a downloader cannot?

The post may be visible through your account permissions while hidden from other visitors. Test its address from a separate browser profile.

Can a downloader unlock a friends-only or group video?

No. A downloader should not bypass Facebook’s audience settings. Ask the owner for permission or retrieve your own post through Facebook’s account-download controls.
