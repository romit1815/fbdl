Privacy Policy — FBviddl

This Privacy Policy explains how FBviddl ("we", "our", or "the service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use any tool on fbviddl.com — including the Facebook Video Downloader, Reel Downloader, Image Downloader, DP Downloader, and Follower Counter. By using FBviddl, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

FBviddl does not require registration. The downloader tools do not ask for your name, email address, or Facebook credentials. The contact form is separate: if you choose to use it, it sends the details you provide to FormSubmit so your message can be delivered to us. Google Analytics and a third-party advertising script also load on some pages; see the sections below for details.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

When you use the downloader tools, FBviddl does not ask you to provide:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you voluntarily submit them through the contact form)
  • Your Facebook account credentials, login tokens, or session data
  • The videos, images, or profile pictures you download through this service
  • Your payment information (FBviddl is and always has been free)
  • Your precise location data

No account is required. Third-party analytics, advertising, and infrastructure providers may receive technical information as described below.

2. Information We Process Temporarily

When you submit a Facebook URL, the page sends it to a Cloudflare Worker to process and return the requested result. Do not include private information in a submitted URL. Cloudflare may process request data, including technical connection information, under its own policies. This site does not use submitted URLs to create user accounts or profiles; the site code does not establish how long infrastructure providers retain request logs.

3. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like most web services, FBviddl's infrastructure may receive standard browser-level request data when you visit the site, including:

  • Your IP address (processed by Cloudflare for routing and DDoS protection, subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)
  • Pages visited on fbviddl.com and approximate timestamps

Google Analytics is configured on the home page, the Hindi home page, and several downloader pages to measure site usage. Google says its default Analytics implementation can collect page interaction, browser and device information, approximate location, and use first-party cookies. A third-party advertising script also loads on the home page. These providers handle information under their own policies; see Google's Privacy Policy and the advertising provider at highrevenueformat.com.

4. Cookies

The follower counter stores the selected Facebook handle, count, initial count, polling total, and recent count history in first-party cookies named fbctr_target, fbctr_count, fbctr_initial, fbctr_ticks, and fbctr_history. The code sets these cookies to expire after seven days so the counter can restore the last session. Google Analytics may also use first-party measurement cookies, and the advertising script may use cookies or similar technologies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser; this may stop the counter from restoring its previous state.

You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent you from using any FBviddl tool.

5. No Media Storage

FBviddl does not provide a media library or ask you to upload files. A submitted link is processed by our Cloudflare Worker and media is returned to your browser. This site does not create a user account or media archive, but the front-end code cannot establish whether infrastructure providers temporarily process or retain request data; see the provider policies above.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

FBviddl uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare Workers — Our backend processing runs on Cloudflare's serverless platform. Cloudflare handles network-level security, DDoS protection, and global routing. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — Measures site usage on the pages where the tag is installed. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising script — The home page loads a script from highrevenueformat.com. The provider may receive browser request data and may use cookies or similar technologies; see highrevenueformat.com.
  • FormSubmit — The contact form sends submitted name, email, subject, and message to FormSubmit for delivery to our email inbox. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days. See its privacy policy and retention information.
  • Content Delivery Networks — Static assets may be served through CDN providers, which receive standard HTTP request headers.

We do not intentionally install social media tracking pixels or use data brokers. The Google Analytics and advertising scripts described above are third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

FBviddl is a general-purpose web utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through this site, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to remove it.

8. Data Security

All connections to fbviddl.com are secured using HTTPS (TLS encryption). This means data transmitted between your browser and our servers cannot be intercepted or read by a third party. Our backend infrastructure is hosted on enterprise-grade infrastructure with access controls, monitoring, and security policies in place. Because we collect minimal data to begin with, the risk surface for data exposure is inherently limited.

9. Your Rights

If you submitted a message through the contact form and want us to delete or correct the copy in our inbox, contact us with enough information to locate it. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days; see its policy for requests relating to its own systems. You can delete first-party cookies through your browser settings. Requests about analytics or advertising data may also need to be directed to the relevant provider.

If you have any concerns about privacy that this policy does not address, you can reach us directly through our Contact page.

10. Changes to This Policy

FBviddl may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, applicable laws, or industry best practices. When significant changes are made, the "Last Updated" date below will be revised. We recommend checking this page periodically. Continued use of FBviddl after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FBviddl handles data, please use our Contact page to reach us. We respond to all legitimate privacy enquiries promptly.

You may also find our Terms of Service relevant for understanding the full conditions under which FBviddl operates.
