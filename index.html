How to save Facebook videos as MP4 files

Three steps to save any public Facebook video to your device. Works with post URLs, page videos, and fb.watch short links.

Step 1: Grab the video URL from Facebook

Locate the public post, timeline video, or fb.watch short link you want to keep. Tap Share → Copy Link on mobile, or copy the full address from your desktop browser bar.

Step 2: Paste the link and start processing

Paste the copied Facebook video URL into the input field at the top of this page. Click the Download button and give the processor a few seconds to locate the video source.

Step 3: Pick a resolution and save the MP4

When the preview appears, choose between the available HD or SD MP4 variants. Your browser will start downloading the video file directly to your device storage.

Facebook Video Downloader

Start with the right link

FBviddl needs the address of one specific public video. There are two ways to get it:

  • In a browser (phone or computer): open the video and copy the URL from the address bar.
  • In the Facebook app: tap Share, then Copy Link.

The address-bar link is the more dependable option. Links from the app's share menu sometimes take longer to resolve or don't load at all. If yours stalls, open the video in your phone's browser and copy the link from there.

A quick test: open the link in a private or incognito window. If the video plays without asking you to log in, it's public and FBviddl should be able to reach it. If Facebook asks you to sign in, it can't.

If the link is a Reel and you want a page built for that, use the Facebook Reel downloader.

What happens after you paste

The page shows a short "processing" message while it looks up the video. Then it displays a preview with two buttons, Download HD and Download SD. The preview lets you confirm you have the right video before you save anything. Click a button and your browser saves the file to its usual download location.

HD or SD?

FBviddl doesn't convert, upscale, or enhance video. It retrieves the file Facebook serves, so your copy can only be as good as the upload. HD gives you the higher-quality version when the original has one. SD is typically the smaller file, which helps on a slow connection or a full phone. A video uploaded in low quality stays low quality whichever button you press.

Saving on your phone or computer

  • Android: the file normally lands in your Downloads folder.
  • iPhone: your phone may ask whether to save to Photos or Files.
  • Windows, Mac or Linux: the file saves through your browser. If nothing starts, right-click the download link and choose "Save link as."

What FBviddl can't download

FBviddl works only with videos anyone can watch without logging in. That excludes friends-only posts, closed or secret groups, and anything behind other privacy settings. FBviddl never asks for your Facebook login, so it can't see anything a logged-out visitor couldn't.

Videos also vanish: if the owner deletes a video or makes it private after sharing the link, the link stops working straight away.

Privacy: what happens to your link and video

This is a summary of the FBviddl privacy policy, so read the full text for detail:

  • No account is needed, and FBviddl doesn't ask for your Facebook credentials.
  • The link you paste is sent to FBviddl's backend, which runs on Cloudflare Workers, to fetch the video. According to the policy, the link isn't kept in any storage system, database or log afterward.
  • The video file is fetched from Facebook's public content network and streamed to your browser. The policy says no copy is written to FBviddl servers.
  • As with any website, standard request data such as IP address, browser and device type passes through Cloudflare for routing and abuse protection.

Only save what you're allowed to use

A video being publicly viewable doesn't mean you can reuse or repost it. The creator generally still holds the copyright.
